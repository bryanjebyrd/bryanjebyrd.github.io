Kettlebell Back Plan

Seven short moves for your neck, upper back, lower back and legs. Watch the moving figure for the rhythm. The glow shows the muscles doing the work.

Keep: the swing

Hinging and snapping the hips trains the glutes and hamstrings, which take load off the lower back. You're doing the right move.

Swap: the upright row to the chin

The narrow grip pulled to the chin tends to pinch the shoulder joint, and it mostly works the upper traps, the same neck muscles that already feel tight. A bent-over row builds the mid-back muscles that hold your posture.

The daily 12 minutes

  1. Swing: 15 to 20 reps
  2. Goblet squat: 10 to 12 reps
  3. One-arm row: 10 each side
  4. Romanian deadlift: 10 reps
  5. Halo: 5 each direction (go light)
  6. Suitcase carry: 30 seconds each side
  7. Chin tucks: 10 reps, no bell

Two rounds on most days. Stop a set when your form slips, not when you hit a number.

If pain shoots down a leg or into an arm, or you feel numbness or tingling, stop and see a doctor or physical therapist before training through it.
